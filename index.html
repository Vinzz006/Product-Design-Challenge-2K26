FIG. 01 About

Building, moving, wiring — innovate together.

The institution and the premise behind Product Design Challenge '26.

01 Meenakshi Sundararajan Engineering College

An autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University, accredited by NBA and AICTE (TNEA Code 1309). This challenge runs under the campus's Institution's Innovation Council, in collaboration with Nodenova, the Renewables Club, the Product Development Club, and the Eco-Design Club.

02 The Challenge

Product Design Challenge '26 is a 30-hour hackathon where teams take on real-world problem statements — not limited to digital systems, power, production, industry, or urban design. Submit your idea, get shortlisted, and build it out over 30 hours on campus.

FIG. 02 How It Works

From blueprint to build, in four stages.

The same arc every team follows across the 30 hours.

01

Ideate

Pick your theme, frame the problem, and sketch the product direction with your team.

02

Build

Design and develop your prototype with mentors checking in throughout.

03

Refine

Test, polish, and prepare a working demo alongside your pitch deck.

04

Pitch

Present your product to the judging panel and answer the hard questions.

FIG. 03 Problem Statements

Five categories to build in.

Not limited to these — bring a strong idea from anywhere and we'll help you place it.

Digital Synergy

Software and connected systems that bring people, devices, and data together.

Power Nexus

Energy generation, storage, and distribution — smarter, cleaner, more efficient.

Smart Production

Automation, robotics, and process innovation for how things get made.

Industry Innovation

Tools and platforms that make existing industries faster, safer, or more capable.

Urban Regeneration

Rethinking city infrastructure, spaces, and services for how people actually live.

FIG. 05 Rules & Regulations

What's expected, in plain terms.

01

Each team must have exactly 4 members — interdisciplinary teams are entertained.

02

All work must be original and developed entirely during the hackathon.

03

Participants must bring their own necessities — laptop, cables, chargers, and so on.

04

Formal attire and a valid ID card are mandatory throughout the event.

05

Shortlisted teams must submit an undertaking form before the event.

06

A fee of ₹250 per member is collected from shortlisted teams only.

⚠ NOTE — Nothing is charged at registration. The ₹250-per-member fee applies only once your team is shortlisted, due by 7 Sep 2026.

FIG. 06 Judging Criteria

How submissions are scored.

A general guide to what the panel looks for — official weightings are set by the judges on the day.

Innovation & Originality

High priority

Is the idea genuinely novel, or a fresh angle on an existing problem?

Technical Execution

High priority

Does the prototype actually work, and how well is it engineered?

Design & User Experience

Key factor

Is it usable, coherent, and thoughtfully put together?

Impact & Feasibility

Key factor

Does it solve a real problem, and could it realistically be built out further?

Pitch & Presentation

Considered

Can the team explain what they built and why it matters, clearly and confidently?

// Bar length reflects relative emphasis, not an official published score split.

FIG. 11 Code of Conduct

A safe, respectful build environment.

Applies to every participant, mentor, judge, volunteer, and organizer, for the full 30 hours.

01

Treat everyone with respect — harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated.

02

Respect venue property, equipment, and quiet hours in shared spaces.

03

Give credit where it's due — plagiarized code, designs, or ideas will result in disqualification.

04

Look out for your teammates — get rest, stay hydrated, and ask organizers for help if you need it.

Report a concern

If something feels wrong, tell any organizer or volunteer immediately — they're identifiable by their event lanyards. All reports are handled promptly and discreetly.

FIG. 12 FAQ

Good to know before you apply.

Who can participate?+
Anyone with basic coding knowledge can take part — you don't need to be an expert to apply.
Can first-year students join?+
Yes. First-year students are welcome to form or join a team.
How do I register?+
Head to the registration page and fill in your team's details — it takes about five minutes.
What should I bring?+
Your laptop and any hardware or cables your build needs.
Are there prizes?+
Yes — there's a ₹15,000 prize pool shared across the standout teams, plus certificates for every participant.
Is there a participation fee?+
Only shortlisted teams pay — a nominal ₹250 per member, collected after selection.
How do I check if my team was shortlisted?+
Head to the Results page and search for your team name or code — it's linked in the nav bar and hero.
